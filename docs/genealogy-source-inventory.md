## Method and limitations

All 49 distinct editable `.ddd` files decode as Diagram Designer raw-deflate CP1256 drawings. Names (including encoded original line breaks), bounding boxes, decompressed-stream byte offsets, source checksums, confidence/review states and connector candidates are retained for each extracted node and relationship. All 49 drawings were visually inspected or compared with their corresponding raster charts; only anchored descendant components from 37 drawings are eligible for this import. Arrow endpoints must touch a drawn node boundary; source order and name proximity never establish a father. Endpoint tolerances are 4,500 drawing units; wire joins use 1,800 units. Ambiguous components and disconnected nodes remain observations. These tolerances are extraction aids, not proof of identity.

PNG charts for Eid (4), Khamis (4), Rahaymeh (4), Qawasmeh (overview plus 7), Awtalat (5), Jumah (2), Hamdallah, the family overview JPG, and both one-page genealogy PDFs were visually inspected. Both PDFs have no useful text layer. The Ahmad PDF was rasterized and compared with its editable source. Awtalat ancestry remains conflicting; no founder or connecting father was guessed. Editable Thalji, Mahmoud, Hamdan and Ali/Wahsh charts and the two QQ overview variants were rendered and inspected. Faqara and Abdul-Latif lack a reliable common anchor; Awtalat remains contradictory. Their candidate relationships are retained without speculative attachment.

The XLSX copies are identical by SHA256. Every worksheet was read, including Branch_Roots, Possible_Duplicates and Review_Conflicts. V4 has 77 root rows, 1,418 nonblank rows without parent IDs, and 10 blocked blank-name rows. No unsupported parent identifiers were synthesized.
